Methodology

Each entry's headline dataset size was checked against the underlying paper/model card for an explicit per-language or Swahili-specific breakdown. Where none existed, we marked it "not disclosed" rather than assuming the headline figure applies to Swahili — most large multilingual corpora (XLS-R, Whisper, MMS, WAXAL) do not publish this.

Key Finding

The clearest documented example is the 2026 CPT study: it draws on a ~200-hour unlabeled Common Voice Swahili pool but needed only 11 hours of labeled data to reach SOTA (3.24% WER) — showing published pool size and actual effective training data can differ by an order of magnitude even within one paper.

Relevance to This App

Our 3 benchmarked engines (Omnilingual, MMS, Whisper tiny) all have undisclosed or zero direct Swahili training data at the per-language level — their Swahili performance relies substantially on cross-lingual transfer from related Bantu/African languages, not dedicated Swahili corpora.
