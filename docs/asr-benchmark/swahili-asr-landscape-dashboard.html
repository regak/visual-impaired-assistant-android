Coverage

13 Swahili-dedicated entries verified against primary sources, spanning 2012 (lineage context) through 2026. Excludes broad multilingual-inclusive models (XLS-R's full 128-language base, WAXAL's full 21-language set) per current scope — flagged for a follow-up pass.

Key Finding

Academic SOTA on Common Voice Swahili improved from 8.3% WER (XLS-R, 2021) to 3.24% WER (continued pretraining, 2026) — a 61% relative gain in 5 years, almost entirely via better pretraining/fine-tuning strategy rather than raw data volume.

Relevance to This App

Our 3 benchmarked engines (Omnilingual, MMS, Whisper tiny) are all general multilingual models, not Swahili-dedicated fine-tunes. Several dedicated models here (w2v-bert-2.0-swahili-asr, CPT approach) report substantially lower WER and may be worth benchmarking directly.
