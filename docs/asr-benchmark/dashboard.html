Summary & notes
  • MMS-1B-all wins on accuracy on 22 of 30 clips and ties/loses on the rest — ~38% lower mean WER and ~40% lower mean CER than the current Omnilingual engine, at ~2.7x the on-device model size and ~37% slower per utterance (single CPU core, no GPU/NNAPI acceleration tested).
  • Whisper tiny is not viable for Swahili — mean WER above 1.0 (more word errors than words spoken), with clear hallucination loops on several clips (e.g. clip 19, clip 20). Ruled out.
  • Even the best engine (MMS) still averages ~33% WER — real-world dictation will still need the user to confirm/correct via the existing readback step; this is an accuracy improvement, not a solved problem.
  • Hardest clips for all three engines were long multi-clause sentences with embedded numbers spoken as words (clips 13, 14, 19, 21, 24) — consistent with the separate Swahili-number-word conversion bug already fixed for the recipient field.
  • Full per-clip transcripts, raw results, test-set manifest and benchmark script are committed at docs/asr-benchmark/ in the repo (commit 8b76da8).
